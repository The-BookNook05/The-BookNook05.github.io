# Idea
I set up meetings with those who wish to broaden their writing/reading skills. Also help aspiring writers advance their skill set and help with any revisions needed. I spend 30-60 minutes during each session either: reading through a story that's been written and give corrections/critique Help students in need of a reading/writing tutor Help with the publishing/editing process

# Why?
I know what it is like to feel as if I have no one to help me with my problems. I have attention deficiency issues and struggle to understand things I wish to understand. I want to be there for others that struggle like I do.

# Who am I?
* I am Storie Hicks
* I am a 21 one year old aspiring author
* I spend most of my days either working or writing/reading
* I have been writing since I was 12 and didn't start actually turning my stories into books until I was fifteen.
  
# Do I get anything out of this besides money?
* Yes I absolutely do. I get to help broaden the minds of the young, the old, and those who have lost complete faith in themselves. I know what it is like to just suddenly not know what or how to write. I struggle with writer's block very often and it becomes hard for me to even find joy in writing or even reading. Being able to help others; big or small, means so much to me and helps me feel accomplished.
